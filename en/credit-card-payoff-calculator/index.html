How the Calculator Works

Debt Snowball vs Debt Avalanche

Snowball prioritizes the smallest balance. Avalanche prioritizes the highest APR. Compare both with the same monthly budget.

Important estimate note

Real issuers may use daily interest, variable APRs, fees and different minimum-payment formulas. Verify decisions against your statement.
